# Coil Cleaning and Wet-Work Discussion Checklist

Educational prompts, not a cleaning procedure, permit, or official record.
Read [Safety and Authorization Boundaries](../SAFETY.md) first. This filename is
retained for existing links; it does not imply permission to use a pressure washer.
Keep job details and permitted photos in approved private systems.

## Plan the Method

- [ ] Exact equipment/coil instructions and any coating-specific restrictions available?
- [ ] Proposed cleaner, pressure, distance, direction, and tools explicitly compatible
  with those instructions? No generic pressure or nozzle-distance rule assumed?
- [ ] Required energy-control procedure, authorized roles, and verification identified?
- [ ] Electrical compartments protected from liquid under the approved method?
  Covers and GFCIs are not substitutes for isolation.
- [ ] Electrical tools suitable for the wet location, with required protection?
- [ ] Chemical label, SDS, task-specific PPE, drainage, and runoff rules reviewed?

## Cleaning Scope

- [ ] Coil type confirmed; no assumption that tube-and-fin methods suit microchannel?
- [ ] Pressure washer avoided unless the exact manufacturer instructions permit it?
- [ ] Chemicals used only where the coil instructions permit them?
- [ ] Dwell and rinse requirements taken from the approved instructions and label?
- [ ] Fins, coatings, sensors, electricals, and nearby workers protected from damage?
- [ ] Drain/pan work limited to the approved method, not improvised pressurization?

## Closeout

- [ ] Protective coverings, tools, water ingress, drainage, and drying reviewed
  under the approved procedure?
- [ ] Safe equipment disposition and any return-to-service authorization confirmed?
- [ ] Remaining concerns and handoff recorded in the official private system?

Some equipment prohibits pressure washers and chemicals outright. See the
model-specific [Johnson Controls TMAL microchannel bulletin TPM-SI0245](https://docs.johnsoncontrols.com/ductedsystems/api/khub/documents/TUg78wHItRmyi4kpDTod4w/content)
and the [electrical safety discussion checklist](electrical-safety.md); do not
treat either as a replacement for the instructions governing the actual unit.
