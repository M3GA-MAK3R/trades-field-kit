# First-Year Review and Career Development Worksheet

A blank preparation aid for a private discussion with your mechanic, mentor, or
supervisor. A first year at one company is not necessarily your first year of
technical experience. This is not an employer evaluation, official training
record, competency sign-off, promotion promise, or authorization for independent work.

Read the [safety boundaries](../docs/SAFETY.md). Keep completed worksheets,
reviewer feedback, employer records, and customer/site information in approved
private systems. Publish blank templates or permitted sanitized examples only.

## Connect HVAC Learning With Software Work

Choose a small, permissioned improvement that supports the crew's work instead
of replacing trade fundamentals, approved procedures, or existing systems.

| Candidate contribution | Evidence to discuss | Boundary to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| Field-reference navigation and structured learning aids | Actual files, review feedback, revisions | Not official forms or safety sign-offs |
| Source-traceable calculations and input validation | Manufacturer references, code, regression results | Tests do not validate every field diagnosis |
| Read-only trend/data-quality learning exercise | Explicitly approved or synthetic data and stated limitations | No production BAS connection, credentials, writes, or control changes |
| Service-note or handoff prototype | Blank/synthetic examples and usability feedback | No employer/client records or unauthorized workflow replacement |

For any claimed result, state the baseline, method, sample/time period, and
limitations. If you did not measure time saved, reduced callbacks, or adoption,
say **not measured**; do not invent a percentage or imply the shop uses the tool.
Ask about employer policies, intellectual property, and permission before using
work time, systems, data, or internal materials for a personal project.

## Agree a Career Direction and Next Steps

Treat these as directions to explore with the reviewer, not roles you are already
qualified for: deeper service/diagnostics, controls/BAS literacy, commissioning
support, or software-enabled service operations. Ask which fits the team's work,
which fundamentals come first, and what demonstrated evidence would support it.

Ask what training, certifications, experience, and employer assessments apply to
the proposed direction; do not infer qualification from time served or software
ability. Distinguish prior technical training from licenses or trade credentials.
If discussing compensation or title, ask for the employer's criteria and process,
not an assumed entitlement. Confirm agreed actions and the next review in the
appropriate private record; do not imply an agreement that was not made.

## Portfolio-Safe Case Study Outline

Publish only your own work and examples you have permission to share. Remove
names, addresses, serials, access information, job photos, proprietary documents,
private feedback, and identifying metadata. Sanitization alone does not establish
permission. Do not imply employer endorsement or field deployment.

- **Problem:** [A general learning/reference friction, without naming a site or coworker.]
- **My contribution:** [What you designed, implemented, reviewed, and tested; identify
  AI-assisted drafting/coding and human review honestly.]
- **Public evidence:** [Permitted repository files, your actual commits, source
  citations, tests, and limitations. Credit collaborators and external sources.]
- **Outcome:** [What actually works or changed; label adoption and impact not measured
  when unknown. A software test pass is not demonstrated trade competence.]
- **Next step:** [A specific skill or evaluation you are pursuing, not a credential claimed.]

Suggested opening to adapt only if accurate:

> I maintain an AI-assisted, open-source HVAC field-reference toolkit to connect
> field learning with source-traceable documentation and software testing.
> It includes discussion aids and calculation tools with explicit safety,
> privacy, and validation limits. It supports learning and professional
> accountability; it does not replace approved procedures or demonstrate
> independent trade authorization.
